Project Overview

The Forward Deployed Engineer project is an AI-powered Context Graph Question Answering system designed for SAP Order-to-Cash datasets. The application combines structured databases, knowledge graphs, graph visualization, and large language models to help users understand enterprise business processes through natural language. Instead of manually searching through thousands of records, users can ask business questions and receive contextual answers generated from verified dataset information.

Problem Statement

Enterprise business systems generate large amounts of transactional data that are difficult to analyze. Business users often depend on reports or technical teams to answer questions about customers, sales orders, deliveries, and billing documents. The goal of this project was to create an intelligent question-answering platform that enables business users to explore enterprise data through natural language while maintaining accuracy and traceability.

How It Works

  • SAP Order-to-Cash datasets are loaded into the platform.
  • The ingestion pipeline processes records and stores them in SQLite fact tables.
  • Relationships between customers, products, sales orders, deliveries, and billing documents are converted into a knowledge graph.
  • Users submit questions through a web interface.
  • The FastAPI backend classifies the request and determines the correct retrieval strategy.
  • Relevant information is fetched using SQL queries and graph traversal techniques.
  • OpenRouter LLM generates contextual answers using retrieved business data.
  • Results are displayed together with graph visualizations and supporting evidence.
